# Changes

## 1.1.1 (27 Nov 2026)
**Fix: genes with the same value in every sample.** In 1.0 and 1.1.0, `anova_group_effect` (used by `geometry_check` and `run_checks`) divided the between-group by the within-group sum of squares without a guard. For a gene with the same value in all samples both are 0 in exact arithmetic (F = 0/0: no group effect). In floating point, when the computed grand mean differed from the value by rounding, the between-group sum of squares was about 1e-30, F was infinite and the gene got p = 0, so it was counted as a group-effect gene; when the grand mean was exact, p was NaN and `_bh` turned every q of the data set into NaN, so no gene was counted. Such genes now get p = 1 (F is reported as NaN), and `_bh` counts a NaN p as 1 instead of propagating it (this also covers the treatment-adjusted q values, where a gene whose residuals are exactly zero gives an undefined correlation). Results change only for data sets that contain such genes. A gene with zero counts in every sample becomes such a gene after the pseudocount that `_check_X` asks for. Two smoke tests were added (12 tests). Found in the pipeline audit of 27 Nov 2026; the archived analysis module of the study (`strengthen_v2/coexp_checks.py`) is unchanged.

**Effect on the study.** One benchmark endpoint of the study was computed with the archived module and keeps its as-run value in the article (Additional file 5, table 4.2, and Additional file 6, sheet `Bench_validation_endpoints`, endpoints `raw_sig_share_group_effect` and `raw_sig_share_above_expectation`; development arrays, gene set A): the share of pooled-significant raw pairs among group-effect genes, median 0.81 (quartiles 0.62 and 0.91) over 35 series, above the expected 0.61 in 27 of 35 series (77%). In GSE28477, set A, one gene has the same value in all 12 samples, so the archived module found no group-effect gene and the series dropped out. With 1.1.1 this series has 16 group-effect genes (share 0.73), and the endpoint is 0.80 (quartiles 0.62 and 0.91) over 36 series, 28 of 36 (78%). No other number of the article changes: the INS-1 matrix and the 118 validation matrices have no such gene, and in GSE20677 (sets A and B) no gene has a group effect with either version.

## 1.1.0 (19 Nov 2026), relative to the archived 1.0 module
The archived module is `CMBL_Sub2/strengthen_v2/coexp_checks.py` of the study repository (release v2.0.0; SHA-256 `7e6c42aa7a27232ae4ff9bae6bbf8b2061abf8ac79352067bdd3f831a6d2b44d`, "1.0 (WP1, 4 Nov 2026)"). Three changes; no existing statistic changed.

1. **New function `group_mean_share_expected`**: the share of the pooled covariance carried by group means against the value expected when within-group coupling is removed (every gene permuted independently within its group; the pooled-significant pairs are recomputed in every permuted matrix). It gives the same numbers as the post hoc script of the study (checked on 8 development matrices and on the INS-1 matrix: equal to 1e-12). `run_checks` returns it as `group_mean_share_expected` and the markdown report prints it.
2. **A warning that was misleading was replaced.** Version 1.0 warned "group means carry X% of the pooled covariance of significant raw pairs: pooled networks mostly restate the design" whenever the share exceeded 0.9. In the study's development series the share was never above what independence gives (a post hoc analysis), so a share near 1 is not by itself evidence about the design. The warning now reports the share, the expected share and its 95% null interval, and says that a share near 1 reflects the group effects of the genes.
3. **A new warning for genes without within-group variation** (pooled within-group SD on the log scale below 1e-6, the rule of the benchmark code). Such genes get residual correlations that are numerical noise; in the study's benchmark they were the cause of the excess type I error of T_r2.

Checks run on this module: `tests/test_smoke.py` (10 known-answer tests, all passed) and the original 56-check validation suite of the study (`test_coexp_checks.py`, run against this module in a copy of the repository layout); the outputs are in `RUN_LOG.txt`. The package was not built or installed in a clean environment (that would download its dependencies).
