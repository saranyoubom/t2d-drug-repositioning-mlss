# WP4 extension: positive control for the islet cross-check and a wider search for matched perturbation data (pre-specified 6 Nov 2026)

**Status.** Written after the primary WP4 result was known (pooled excess E +0.013, 95% CI -0.052 to +0.078; decision rule of protocol 4.4: "no meaningful excess co-expression") and after the user asked for (1) a positive control with better wording and (2) a wider search for matched perturbation data, with the stated hope of a more supportive cross-check. Neither part changes the primary analysis, the margin of +/-0.10, the datasets, the donors or the decision rule of 4.4, and neither may replace them. Everything below is fixed BEFORE any of its analyses was run (fingerprint in `../PROTOCOL_v2_SHA256.txt`); results are reported whichever way they point, a result against the INS-1 findings in the same way as one for them; any later change is a deviation logged in protocol section 8 with a time. Not outward-facing: no commit, push or upload.

## A. Positive control: what could the cross-check have detected?
Question: if the evaluable consensus genes were truly coupled in beta-cells, would this cross-check have said so?

**A1. Synthetic spike-in into the real cells** (`positive_control.py`).
* Data: the six analyses of `results/` (five human datasets, the mouse dataset alone), primary variant (all frozen beta-cells), depth-adjusted mode, evaluable genes and consensus pairs exactly as in the primary run.
* Genes injected: the distinct genes of the evaluable consensus pairs of that dataset; all share ONE latent factor per replicate, so every pair among them is coupled (the consensus pairs are a subset of those pairs).
* Scale: donor-centred log-normalized expression of the eligible genes (`wp4_core.lognorm`, `center_by_donor`), before the depth adjustment.
* Factor: z_i ~ N(0, 1) per cell, centred on the donor mean and scaled to unit variance; loading lambda_g = sqrt(rho / (1 - rho)) x sd_g, with sd_g the SD of the donor-centred values of gene g over all cells; nominal rho in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5}.
* Modes: "detected" (PRIMARY): lambda_g z_i is added only to cells in which the gene is detected (raw value > 0), so zeros (dropout) stay zeros; "all" (secondary, an optimistic upper bound): added to every cell. After the injection the values are re-centred on the donor mean.
* Pipeline: unchanged (rank of the residuals after regression on donor-centred depth and depth squared, Spearman; excess E = mean Fisher z of the evaluable pairs minus the mean over the 2000 matched random pair sets of the primary run, with the same draws, because the null genes are not injected; SE of E as in the primary run). Gate: with no injection the code must reproduce the primary E of `results/index.csv` to 1e-9.
* Replicates: 200 per dataset, rho and mode; seeds `numpy.random.SeedSequence([2029, dataset index, rho index, mode index, replicate])`, dataset index 0 to 5 in the order gse81608, gse84133_human, gse86469, gse81547, emtab5061, gse84133_mouse.
* Per replicate the five human datasets are pooled with the primary estimator (REML tau-squared, Hartung-Knapp, margin +/-0.10, the verdict words of 4.4); the mouse dataset is read alone.
* Reported: recovered excess (mean and SD of E over replicates) and the attenuation (mean of E_replicate - E_primary divided by atanh(rho)) per dataset; per dataset the share of replicates with empirical P of E below 0.05; pooled verdict shares ("no meaningful excess", "inconclusive", "co-expressed more") per rho and mode.
* **Reading rule (fixed now).** R1: the cross-check is called SENSITIVE if, at nominal rho 0.3 in mode "detected", the pooled verdict is NOT "no meaningful excess co-expression" in at least 80% of the replicates (false-reassurance rate 20% or less). The manuscript may then say that a coupling of nominal strength 0.3 among the evaluable consensus genes would have been detected. R2: if R1 fails, the manuscript states the smallest nominal rho of the grid with a false-reassurance rate of 20% or less (or "not reached up to 0.5") and words the primary result as "no excess detectable at this sensitivity". Mode "all" is an upper bound and is never used for R1 or R2.

**A2. Real-gene positive control: ribosomal protein genes** (same script).
* Gene list (fixed): cytosolic ribosomal protein genes RPL3 RPL4 RPL5 RPL6 RPL7 RPL7A RPL8 RPL9 RPL10 RPL10A RPL11 RPL12 RPL13 RPL13A RPL14 RPL15 RPL17 RPL18 RPL18A RPL19 RPL21 RPL22 RPL23 RPL23A RPL24 RPL26 RPL27 RPL27A RPL28 RPL29 RPL30 RPL31 RPL32 RPL34 RPL35 RPL35A RPL36 RPL37 RPL37A RPL38 RPL39 RPLP0 RPLP1 RPLP2 RPS2 RPS3 RPS3A RPS4X RPS5 RPS6 RPS7 RPS8 RPS9 RPS10 RPS11 RPS12 RPS13 RPS14 RPS15 RPS15A RPS16 RPS17 RPS18 RPS19 RPS20 RPS21 RPS23 RPS24 RPS25 RPS26 RPS27 RPS27A RPS28 RPS29 RPSA FAU UBA52 (mouse: the same names in mouse capitalization; Entrez and Ensembl ids for GSE81608 and GSE86469 from the NCBI Datasets gene report).
* Per dataset: RP genes detected in at least 10% of the frozen beta-cells; 100 random sets of 11 pairs of distinct RP genes (seed 2030 + dataset index); E_RP per set as in 4.4 (depth-adjusted; each pair's two genes matched to detection deciles; null genes drawn from the pool WITHOUT any RP gene and without the 17 genes; 2000 draws). Reported: mean and SD of E_RP over the sets, share of sets with E_RP at least 0.10, per dataset.
* Reading rule: the statistic is called able to detect real co-expression if the mean E_RP exceeds +0.10 in at least 4 of the 5 human datasets. Ribosomal co-expression is strong; this control says nothing about the strength expected for regulatory modules.

**A3. Margin sensitivity (descriptive).** For margins 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 the verdict of the primary pooled interval is listed together with the smallest margin for which the verdict is "no meaningful excess" (the larger of the absolute interval limits). The pre-set margin of 4.4 stays +/-0.10.

## B. Wider search for matched perturbation data
**B1. Sources and queries** (`wider_search.py`; each run once, counts and date recorded in `wider_search/queries.json`, every returned record listed in `wider_search/candidates_all.csv` with the source that returned it). Term groups (ASCII only):
* CELL = islet OR islets OR "beta cell" OR "beta cells" OR "beta-cell" OR INS-1 OR INS1 OR "INS-1E" OR MIN6 OR EndoC OR betaTC OR "BRIN-BD11" OR RINm5F OR insulinoma OR "pancreatic beta"
* NOTCH = Notch OR "gamma-secretase" OR "gamma secretase" OR DAPT OR LY411575 OR RO4929097 OR "compound E" OR MK-0752 OR RBPJ OR Jagged OR Jag1 OR Dll1 OR Dll4 OR Notch1 OR Notch2
* WNT = Wnt OR DKK1 OR Dickkopf OR "beta-catenin" OR CTNNB1 OR TCF7L2 OR TCF4 OR IWP OR "IWP-2" OR IWR OR XAV939 OR LGK974 OR "ICG-001" OR CHIR99021 OR GSK3 OR Wnt3a OR Axin OR SFRP OR Frizzled
* S1, NCBI GEO DataSets (db=gds): `gse[ETYP] AND (NOTCH OR WNT) AND (CELL)`.
* S2, EBI BioStudies / ArrayExpress search API, the same three term groups as one query; if the service answers with an error or a challenge page the source is reported as not available and not worked around.
* S3, PubMed: `(CELL) AND (NOTCH OR WNT) AND (transcriptom* OR "RNA-seq" OR "RNA sequencing" OR microarray OR "expression profiling" OR "gene expression")`; all PMIDs (at most the first 1500 by PMID), linked to GEO series by E-utilities elink (pubmed_gds); each linked series is added with its PMID.

**B2. Keep rules** (applied to title, summary, design text and sample records only; no expression value is read before the freeze).
* K1 model: expression profiling (array or sequencing) of islets, beta-cell lines or sorted or beta-cell-derived cells of human, mouse or rat; stem-cell or progenitor differentiation, reprogramming, whole tissues other than isolated islets, single-cell atlases without a perturbation, and non-expression assays are dropped.
* K2 perturbation and expected sign: inhibition of Notch (gamma-secretase inhibitors, RBPJ, Notch or ligand loss, neutralizing antibodies) matched to the DAPT arm and inhibition of Wnt (DKK1, beta-catenin, TCF7L2 or TCF4 loss, porcupine or tankyrase inhibitors) matched to the DKK-1 arm, expected sign +1; MIRROR designs (Notch gain of function; Wnt activation by ligand, GSK3 inhibition, Sfrp, Axin or APC loss) are kept with expected sign -1 (their fold changes are multiplied by -1 before the comparison) and flagged "mirror".
* K3 replication: at least 3 samples per arm (arms and contrast fixed at the freeze: perturbed arm = the highest dose or strongest perturbation at the time point nearest 24 h; several independent perturbations of the same pathway are pooled as one perturbed arm; control arm = vehicle, wild type or non-targeting control of the same system and time; a paired design is paired by donor or animal when the sample records give the pairing).
* K4 data: processed expression values or counts obtainable by plain download (series matrix with a values table, NCBI-generated RNA-seq counts, or a processed supplementary table with gene identifiers); checked from file lists and sizes only. A kept series whose data cannot be mapped to genes by a generic parser (platform annotation from GEO, Entrez or Ensembl ids, gene symbols) is reported "not analysable" and stays on the list.
* K5 genes: at least 12 of the 17 genes must be represented (checked at the analysis step; failures are reported, not removed from the list).
* GSE60803 (kept in the first search, contrast fixed in addendum G of `PRESPEC_WP4_choices.md`) is part of the set.

**B3. Freeze.** Every candidate with its decision, reason, expected sign, mirror flag and contrast is written to `wider_search/frozen_candidates.csv` and fingerprinted (sha256 appended to `../PROTOCOL_v2_SHA256.txt`) BEFORE any expression value of a candidate is read.

**B4. Analysis.** Per kept and analysable series: log2 fold change of each of the 17 genes (arrays: difference of the arm means on the log2 scale, linear matrices are log2(x + 1) transformed when the maximum exceeds 50; probe set with the highest mean control expression per gene; RNA-seq: difference of the arm means of log2(CPM + 1); orthologs by Entrez id or by symbol: human upper case, mouse and rat with the INS-1 capitalization, Glut2 = SLC2A2 or Slc2a2, Ins1 = INS in human and Ins1 in mouse and rat), multiplied by the expected sign, compared with the INS-1 log2 fold change of the matching arm (`../wp5_reporting/effect_sizes.csv`: RT-qPCR, "as reported"; DAPT vs CTRL for Notch designs, DKK-1 vs CTRL for Wnt designs): genes with both fold changes nonzero, exact two-sided binomial test of the number of equal signs against 0.5, Spearman rho and its P.

**B5. Pooling and decision rule (fixed now).** All analysable series with at least 12 genes: random-effects pooling (REML, Hartung-Knapp, t on k - 1 df) of the Fisher z of the Spearman rho with variance 1.06 / (n genes - 3). PRIMARY: all series together; secondary: Notch series and Wnt series separately, mirror designs excluded, GSE60803 excluded. Outcome words: pooled 95% CI entirely above 0 = "concordant with the INS-1 arm"; entirely below 0 = "discordant"; otherwise "inconclusive"; with fewer than 3 analysable series the rule is not applied and only the per-series results are reported. Also reported: the share of equal signs among the INS-1 genes with BH q < 0.05 in the matching arm (`bh_q_17genes` of `effect_sizes.csv`).

## C. Wording
The proposed text changes for the abstract, Results, Discussion, Limitations and Conclusions are written AFTER A and B, in `WORDING_islet_crosscheck_v3.md`, from the numbers of the result files and the reading rules above. They describe the cross-check as consistent or inconsistent with the treatment-structure conclusion of the paper and say what it cannot test; they do not claim that it supports co-regulation or synergy unless B5 says "concordant".

**Framing (decided with the user, 6 Nov, before any analysis of this note ran).** The network and drug-ranking parts are worded as computational, data-driven, hypothesis-generating results from one preparation ("identifies", "ranks", "prioritizes", "is consistent with"); "validates", "confirms", "synergy" and "co-regulation" are not used unless B5 says "concordant" and even then only for the specific comparison made. The wet-lab readouts are described as observations of a single experiment. Wet-lab confirmation is described as the next step, PROPOSED and not done: independent biological replicates (design table `../wp2_benchmark2/design_table.md`: with 6 to 8 independent experiments per group T_r2 detects module coupling at r = 0.5 with power 0.78 to 0.96; 12 to 20 per group are needed to detect 52% to 95% of true module pairs at r = 0.5) and dose-response combination matrices (Bliss and Loewe reference models) for top and bottom MLSS pairs. `WORDING_islet_crosscheck_v3.md` carries a section "Wet-lab confirmation (proposed)".
