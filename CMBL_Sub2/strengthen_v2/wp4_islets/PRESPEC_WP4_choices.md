Protocol v2 section 4 stays binding. This note only fills the details the protocol leaves open. It was written after the gate (4.1) had passed and after the beta-cell lists were frozen (`frozen_beta_cells.csv`), and before (a) any expression value of GSE86469, GSE81547 or E-MTAB-5061 was read, (b) any depth-adjusted statistic of any dataset was computed, (c) any meta-analysis or metacell number existed. Only file layouts (header lines, row names) were looked at. Fingerprint in `../PROTOCOL_v2_SHA256.txt`. Any change after this point is a deviation and goes into protocol section 8 with a time.

| dataset | file | kind | gene label | library size (depth, counts) | detected genes (depth, RPKM) |

* Normalization for the correlations: UMI log2(CP10K + 1); read or expected counts log2(CPM + 1); RPKM log2(RPKM + 1), as in `wp4_core.lognorm` (gate-verified).
* Depth covariate (protocol 4.2): log10 library size for UMI and read-count data; log10 number of detected genes for RPKM data. For E-MTAB-5061 the counts block is used (not the RPKM block) so that the library size is defined; this is chosen before the file was opened.
* Evaluable genes: detected (value > 0) in at least 10% of the frozen beta-cells of that dataset; identical in the unadjusted and the adjusted analysis. The matched-null pool is every evaluable gene that is not one of the 17, deciles of detection, 2000 draws, seeds 2026 and 2027, as in the gate.
* All frozen beta-cells enter the primary analysis, including cells from donors with type 2 diabetes (GSE86469, E-MTAB-5061, GSE81608, GSE84133 donor human4). Sensitivity for every dataset that has such donors: non-diabetic donors only, same rules.

## B. Primary endpoint (protocol 4.4)
* Analysis entering the meta-analysis: the depth-adjusted one (`mode = adjusted`); the unadjusted one is the sensitivity analysis.
* Qualifying human datasets: at least 3 evaluable consensus pairs at the 10% rule. Rule applied only to datasets that qualify. Mouse (GSE84133) is reported alone.
* Study effect E (excess) and SE_E as defined in `wp4_core.analyse` (mean Fisher z of the evaluable consensus pairs minus the mean over the 2000 matched random pair sets; SE = SD of those 2000 means). Margin +/-0.10 is applied on the E scale (Fisher z; atanh(0.10) = 0.1003, so the scales agree to three decimals).
* Pooling: tau-squared by REML, Hartung-Knapp adjusted standard error, t quantile with k - 1 degrees of freedom, no floor on the variance factor. This is the primary interval for the rule. Reported beside it as sensitivity (never used for the rule): the same with the Knapp-Hartung factor floored at 1 (IntHout 2014), DerSimonian-Laird tau-squared with the Hartung-Knapp interval, DerSimonian-Laird with the normal interval, fixed effect; leave-one-dataset-out; non-diabetic donors only; unadjusted E.
* Outcome words are the protocol's: CI inside [-0.10, +0.10] = no meaningful excess co-expression; CI entirely above +0.10 = co-expressed more than random pairs; anything else = inconclusive. With fewer than 2 qualifying datasets the rule is not applied.
* Pair-level pooled rho (pairs evaluable in at least 3 human datasets): per dataset Fisher z of the depth-adjusted partial Spearman rho, variance 1.06 / (df - 1) with df = cells - donors - 4 (Fieller factor for rank correlation), same random-effects estimator as above, back-transformed to rho with the 95% interval.

## C. Metacell sensitivity (protocol 4.5)
* Donors with at least 40 beta-cells; per partition the cells of a donor are shuffled and cut into consecutive blocks of exactly `size` cells (size 20 primary; 10 and 50 sensitivity); the remainder (fewer than `size` cells) is dropped; a donor needs at least 2 blocks to contribute. 20 partitions per size, partition i uses `numpy.random.default_rng(5000 + i)` over the donors in the order of first appearance.
* Metacell value: UMI and counts summed, then log2(CP10K + 1) (UMI) or log2(CPM + 1) (counts); RPKM averaged, then log2(mean + 1). Donor-centred, ranked, Spearman, no depth term (protocol). Evaluable = nonzero in at least 50% of the metacells; the matched null is rebuilt on the metacell data (pool = evaluable genes, deciles of metacell detection, 2000 draws, seeds 2026 and 2027).
* Reported per dataset and size: number of metacells, evaluable genes, evaluable consensus pairs, median rho and E (median and range over the 20 partitions), and the gain in evaluable consensus pairs against the cell-level analysis.

## D. Perturbation concordance (protocol 4.6)
* Two NCBI GEO DataSets (`db=gds`) queries, run once, results listed in full in `perturbation/candidates.csv` with the decision and reason:
  * Notch: `gse[ETYP] AND (Notch OR "gamma-secretase" OR "gamma secretase" OR DAPT) AND (islet OR islets OR "beta cell" OR "beta cells" OR "beta-cell" OR INS-1 OR INS1 OR MIN6 OR "pancreatic beta")`
  * Wnt: `gse[ETYP] AND (DKK1 OR Dickkopf OR "Wnt inhibitor" OR "beta-catenin" OR TCF7L2 OR TCF4 OR "Wnt signaling" OR "Wnt signalling") AND (islet OR islets OR "beta cell" OR "beta cells" OR "beta-cell" OR INS-1 OR INS1 OR MIN6 OR "pancreatic beta")`
* Kept when: expression profiling of islets, beta-cell lines or beta-cell-derived cells, a perturbation of the Notch (gamma-secretase) or Wnt (DKK1, Wnt inhibitor, beta-catenin or TCF7L2 loss) pathway against a control, and at least 3 samples per arm on a platform that covers the 17 genes. Dropped: stem-cell or progenitor differentiation designs, other organisms of tissue, designs without a control arm, non-expression assays. The decision uses the series title, summary and design text only.
* Log2 fold change of a gene = mean log2 expression in the perturbed arm minus the control arm (paired by donor when the design is paired); several probe sets of a gene are collapsed to the one with the highest mean control expression; the sign is compared with the INS-1 log2 fold change of the matching arm (`../wp5_reporting/effect_sizes.csv`: DAPT arm for Notch, DKK-1 arm for Wnt; reported analysis, Gapdh as reported), exact two-sided binomial test of the sign agreement against 0.5 on genes with both fold changes nonzero, and Spearman correlation of the two vectors. The model systems differ (rat cell line versus the series' cells); this is descriptive.

## F. Addendum 6 Nov 2026 13:5x (E-MTAB-5061 layout; written after reading the IDF description of the data file, before any count value of the file was read)
The IDF of E-MTAB-5061 documents the file `pancreas_refseq_rpkms_counts_3514sc.txt`: two annotation columns (gene symbol, RefSeq id), then the 3514 RPKM columns, then the 3514 read-count columns in the same sample order as the first line; rows 1-26179 are RefSeq genes, the next 92 rows are ERCC spike-ins, the last row is eGFP. Refinement of section A (the table there said "all rows"): library size and number of detected genes of E-MTAB-5061 are taken over the 26179 RefSeq gene rows only (ERCC and eGFP rows excluded, as ERCC rows are excluded for GSE81547). Rows sharing a gene symbol are summed. Cell identity check while streaming (not a selection step): the mean INS read count of the frozen beta-cells must exceed twice the mean of every other author-labelled cell type, and the mean GCG count of alpha cells twice that of the other types (the same kind of check as the earlier GSE84133 loader); if it fails the column alignment is wrong and the dataset is reported as failed, not repaired by trial.

### F2. Correction of the identity-check criterion, 6 Nov 2026 13:5x (after the first parser run stopped; no correlation or other analysis statistic of E-MTAB-5061 had been computed)
The check of addendum F compared the own-type mean with ALL other author-labelled types. It stopped the parser: INS mean count of beta cells 51329 against 36869 for 'co-expression cell' and 12989 for 'unclassified endocrine cell'. These two are the authors' categories of cells that carry several hormones by definition, so the criterion, not the column alignment, was wrong: against every single-hormone type the beta-cell INS mean is at least 134 times higher (largest other: delta 382), and the alpha-cell GCG mean is at least 126 times higher than every other single-hormone type (largest other: ductal 760; per-type means in logs/identity_check_E-MTAB-5061_first_version_all_types.json). Criterion now: the own-type mean must exceed twice the mean of every other author-labelled type except 'co-expression cell', 'unclassified endocrine cell' and 'not applicable' (no type). The frozen beta-cell list is unchanged. The first version of the result file is kept as logs/identity_check_E-MTAB-5061_first_version_all_types.json.

## G. Addendum 6 Nov 2026 14:3x (GSE60803 design; written after reading the sample records of the series matrix, before any expression value of the series was read)
Sample records (4 donors, 12 arrays): untreated (S108, S109, S112), nontarget shRNA (S114), beta-catenin shRNA 9 (S108, S109, S112, S114), beta-catenin shRNA 45 (S108, S109, S114 twice). The records describe the untreated and nontarget arrays as "intact islet cells" and the beta-catenin shRNA arrays as "de-differentiated cells", and the treatment protocol says the shRNA viruses were applied to expanded cells (passages 5-7). A nontarget-shRNA control exists for ONE donor only.
Fixed analysis (protocol 4.6, "paired by donor when the design is paired"): per donor, control = the untreated array (S108, S109, S112) or the nontarget array (S114); perturbed = mean of that donor's beta-catenin shRNA arrays (9 and 45, both replicates of S114); log2 fold change of a gene = mean over the 4 donors of (perturbed - control) on the log2 scale of the series matrix. Several probe sets of a gene: the one with the highest mean control expression. This contrast cannot separate the effect of the beta-catenin knockdown from expansion and dedifferentiation, because the controls are not expanded; it is reported as CONFOUNDED and descriptive. Secondary contrast, descriptive only (control n = 1, below the 3-sample rule): donor S114 beta-catenin shRNA arrays against the S114 nontarget array.
Other series: none met the keep rules (perturbation/candidates_decisions.csv). Sfrp5 silencing (GSE44045, GSE44046; rat beta-cells, Wnt activation) is the opposite direction and is not analysed.
