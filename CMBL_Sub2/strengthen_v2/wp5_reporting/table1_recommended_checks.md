# Table 1. Recommended checks before reading a pooled co-expression network (draft, plain text)

Title (15 words): Recommended checks for pooled co-expression networks from small perturbation experiments, with the evidence found here.

Every number below comes from a file named in the last column; INS-1 numbers are post hoc and from one preparation; benchmark numbers are from public data (37 development series, 40 held-out arrays, 19 human RNA-seq series).

| Check | What it tests | How it is done here | Failure it catches | Evidence in this study (number; file) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Share of pooled covariance carried by group means | Whether the pooled correlation is mostly the treatment design | Covariance of the pooled data split into group-mean and within-group parts | A network that only recovers which groups differ | Group means carry a median 98% [96, 99] of covariance among differentially expressed genes (59 validation sets, 98% [95, 99]); INS-1 about 97%; wp2_benchmark2/validation_summary.md; wp1_ins1_structure/README.md |
| 2. Permutation null of the consensus count, and its sensitivity | Whether the number of consensus pairs exceeds what group structure alone gives | Group-preserving permutation of the genes; the count is compared with its null | Reading "within the null" as proof that nothing else is present: the test is insensitive | INS-1 21 pairs against a null mean 22.1 (P(null >= 21) 0.69); the count test detected injected coupling in 28% of cases at r = 0.7 with 6 coupled genes (68% at r = 0.9); wp2_benchmark2/calibration_tables.csv |
| 3. Calibrated within-group coupling test (T_r2) | Whether genes co-vary within groups beyond independence | Mean squared off-diagonal correlation of within-group residuals, permutation P (2,000 permutations); calibrated on decoupled public data | Hidden within-group structure (batch, run, shared reference) that the count test misses | Power 91% (r = 0.7, 6 genes), 43% (r = 0.5); type I error 7.5% as pre-specified (limit 6%, failed because of one degenerate series), 5.0% without it; INS-1 P = 0.061 (T_p 0.031); coupling in 80% (47/59) of public validation sets; wp2_benchmark2/calibration_verdict.txt; wp1_ins1_structure/tbl_2_6_coupling.csv |
| 4. Treatment adjustment on the raw scheme | Whether pairs survive after removing group means | Residualize on group (df = 8), BH within the scheme | Pairs that are the treatment effect only | INS-1 raw scheme 0 pairs (null mean 0.07); public sets: median 0% of consensus pairs keep q < 0.05 on the raw scheme; wp1_ins1_structure/tbl_2_5_adjusted_calibrated.csv; wp2_benchmark2/validation_summary.md |
| 5. Ratio-scheme (shared denominator) calibration | Whether adjusted counts of ratio-normalized data exceed their own null | Same count compared with its permutation null from the same scheme | Counts inflated by the denominator shared by all ratios | INS-1 adjusted counts 51, 11, 15 against null means 29.3, 11.3, 6.5 (P 0.079, 0.44, 0.078); public sets: median observed/null 0.96-1.42; wp1_ins1_structure/tbl_2_5_adjusted_calibrated.csv; wp2_benchmark2/validation_summary.md |
| 6. Run-structure test | Whether coupling is confined to genes measured in the same run | Same-run against between-run pairs, run-block permutation | Plate, run or reference effects read as co-regulation | INS-1 within-group r among same-run pairs +0.537 (26 Aug), +0.469 (9 Nov), +0.014 (24 June); same-run pairs P 0.020, between-run 0.42; 15 of 21 consensus pairs involve a 26 Aug gene; wp1_ins1_structure/README.md |
| 7. Reference-gene and scale variants | Whether the network survives other normalizations and scales | Gapdh replaced by run-and-group means; no normalization; log-scale Pearson and Spearman | Networks that depend on the reference handling | 18 of 21 pairs kept with run-and-group means (27 consensus pairs, hubs change); 8 of 21 without normalization; hub list differs on every scale; wp1_ins1_structure/tbl_2_8_scale_sensitivity.csv |
| 8. Public single-cell cross-check | Whether the consensus pairs are co-expressed in independent human beta-cells beyond expression-matched random pairs | Donor-centred, depth-adjusted partial Spearman correlation in beta-cells; excess over 2000 matched random pair sets; random-effects meta-analysis across datasets against a margin set in advance | Pairs specific to one in vitro experiment; correlations that only reflect sequencing depth or sparse detection | Pooled excess +0.013 (95% CI -0.052 to +0.078; margin +/-0.10; 5 human datasets; I2 71%): no meaningful excess; 3 of 5 leave-one-out intervals inconclusive; only 11 of 21 pairs evaluable and none of the 7 Wnt pairs; one qualifying perturbation series of 43 (confounded), 7 of 17 genes same sign as the INS-1 DKK-1 arm (P 0.63); wp4_islets/README.md, wp4_islets/meta/meta_report.md |
| 9. Sensitivity of a drug-combination score and a potency-only baseline | Whether a network-derived ranking depends on the network | Scenario recomputation, permuted potency and target mapping | Rankings that only restate potency and target class | MLSS vs mean pIC50 rho 0.9967 among cross-target pairs; class plus potency explains 99% of rank variance; Gapdh variants rho 1.000; permuting potency gives rho 0.755 (0.648-0.871); wp3_mlss/mlss_scenarios.csv |
| 10. Effect sizes with intervals, across-gene correction | Whether reported changes are large and precise | log2 fold change with 95% interval; BH across the 17 genes | Significance without size; one-preparation overstatement | Hes1 under DAPT log2 FC -2.87 [-3.06, -2.68]; DNA content +1.12 [0.02, 2.23] (DAPT), lower limit near zero; 14 genes q < 0.05 for DAPT; wp5_reporting/effect_sizes.md |
| 11. Design guidance | Whether the design can see a module at all | Simulation: 17 genes, 6-gene module, 3 groups | Experiments too small to detect coupled pairs | With 4 per group T_r2 power 0.87 at r = 0.7 but 6% of true pairs detected; the best pair needs |r| >= 0.90; 12 per group give 52% pair detection at r = 0.5; wp2_benchmark2/design_table.md |
