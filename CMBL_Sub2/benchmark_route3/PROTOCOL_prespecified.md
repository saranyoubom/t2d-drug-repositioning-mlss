# Route 3: does the pooled co-expression pipeline mostly recover the design? A pre-specified benchmark on public perturbation datasets

Written **before** any benchmark expression data were downloaded or analysed (only the INS-1 data of the manuscript were used to test the code, see "Code validation"). Any later change is listed under "Deviations". File fingerprint: `PROTOCOL_SHA256.txt`.

## 1. Question
The manuscript argues that, in a 3-group x 4-replicate perturbation design, gene pairs that are significant under all four normalization schemes in the pooled analysis (consensus pairs) are what the group structure alone produces, and that they largely disappear after adjustment for group. Is this general? Across independent public perturbation datasets with the same design, how often is the pooled consensus count explained by a group-preserving permutation null, and how many consensus pairs survive treatment adjustment?

## 2. Pipeline (identical to the manuscript; `reanalysis_17genes/pipeline.py` re-implemented without hard-coded genes)
Input: 12 observations (3 groups x 4 replicates) x 17 genes on the **linear** expression scale. Three of the 17 genes serve as normalization denominators (as Hes1, Kcnj11, Ins1 do in the manuscript). Four schemes: raw expression and the ratio to each denominator gene (that gene is dropped from its own scheme). Pearson r with t-test p for every pair (136 pairs raw, 120 per ratio scheme). **Consensus pair** = p <= 0.05 in all four schemes (primary: any sign; secondary: same sign). **Permutation null** (2000 permutations): on the log scale keep each gene's three group means and permute its within-group residuals independently across genes, rebuild the four schemes, count consensus pairs. **Treatment adjustment**: residualize each scheme on group, partial correlation with df = 12 - 2 - 2 = 8, Benjamini-Hochberg within each scheme.

## 3. Datasets (selected blind to expression values)
* Universe: GEO series (NCBI GEO DataSets index) on one platform each: **GPL570** (human, Affymetrix U133 Plus 2) and **GPL1261** (mouse, Affymetrix 430 2.0); 12 to 36 samples; processed values in the series matrix.
* Candidate list: the series returned by the fixed query in `select_datasets.py`, shuffled with seed 2026; the first 150 per platform are screened **on sample metadata only** (titles and characteristics, never expression values).
* Eligibility from metadata: a perturbation key among the sample characteristics (key contains one of: treatment, agent, compound, drug, stimulus, stimulation, condition, genotype, knockdown, knockout, sirna, shrna, transfection, infection, cytokine, ligand, inhibitor, vehicle, dose, protocol, variation) with **at least 3 values that each have at least 4 samples**; all samples on the single platform. If no characteristic qualifies, groups are inferred from sample titles by removing a trailing replicate token (rep, replicate, #, -, _ followed by a number or letter); otherwise the series is ineligible.
* Final list: the ordered list of eligible series (up to 150 screened per platform) is frozen in `frozen_datasets.csv` with its SHA-256 before any expression data are read. Series are processed in that order. A series that is not analysable for gene set A (fewer than 34 differentially expressed genes, see 3b) or fails a technical check (unreadable file, platform not annotated, fewer than 12 usable samples) is reported with its reason and replaced by the next eligible series, until **20 series per platform are analysable** or the list is exhausted (target 40 in total). The replacement rule uses only the number of differentially expressed genes and file integrity, never a correlation result.
* Groups: the control-like group (label contains control, vehicle, dmso, untreated, mock, ctrl, nt, scrambled, wild, wt, naive, none) if present, else the group whose label sorts first; then the next two groups in order of first appearance. Replicates: the first 4 samples of each group in file order.

## 3b. Per-dataset processing
Values: if the 99th percentile of all values < 30 the matrix is treated as log2 and back-transformed (2^x), otherwise as linear. Probes mapped to the first gene symbol of the platform annotation (probes without a symbol dropped); one probe per gene (highest mean linear expression over the 12 samples). Expressed genes = mean log2 expression above the median across genes. Differential expression = one-way ANOVA across the 3 groups per gene, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR < 0.05.
* **Gene set A (primary, "DE-17")**: 17 genes drawn at random (seed = 2026 + GSE number) from the expressed genes with FDR < 0.05; requires >= 34 such genes, otherwise the dataset is not analysable for A (reported).
* **Gene set B (secondary, "RAND-17")**: 17 genes drawn at random from the expressed genes regardless of DE.
* Denominators = the first three genes of the random draw. Permutation seed = 2026 + GSE number.

## 4. Endpoints
* **E1 (primary)**: per dataset, one-sided permutation P = P(null consensus count >= observed) for gene set A. Reported: proportion of datasets with P >= 0.05 ("pooled count explained by design") with Wilson 95% CI; distribution of P; z = (observed - null mean)/null SD.
* **E2**: share of the observed consensus pairs that remain significant (q < 0.05) after treatment adjustment in at least one scheme and in all four schemes; number of pairs significant in all four schemes after adjustment. Median [IQR] across datasets.
* **E3**: share of the pooled covariance carried by the three group means, among raw-scheme significant pairs (median per dataset).
* Same endpoints for gene set B (secondary).
* **Interpretive bands (fixed now)**: proportion with P >= 0.05 of >= 80% = "pooled consensus counts are generally explained by the design"; 50-80% = "often explained"; < 50% = "generally not explained, within-group coupling is common, so the INS-1 finding is a property of that dataset and not of the design".

## 5. Positive control (sensitivity of the test)
In every gene-set-A analysis, within-group coupling is injected into 6 of the 17 non-denominator genes: one standard-normal latent value per observation (12 values), multiplied by 1.5 x the gene's pooled within-group SD on the log scale, is added to each of the 6 genes (within-group correlation among them about 0.7). Report the proportion of datasets in which E1 flags it (P < 0.05). If this is < 80%, "not above the null" is weak evidence and will be stated as such.

## 6. Code validation (done on the manuscript's own data, before the benchmark)
The generalized code must reproduce, from the 17-gene INS-1 matrix: 21 consensus pairs (any sign), significant pairs per scheme (raw 66, Ins1-normalized 80, Kcnj11-normalized 84, Hes1-normalized 120), a permutation null with mean 22.1 (within 0.5) and P(null >= observed) about 0.69, and the treatment-adjusted counts given by `reanalysis_17genes/pipeline_17genes.json`. The code is not used on benchmark data until it does.

## 7. Reporting
All screened series, all exclusions with reasons, all per-dataset results, every deviation. Downloaded files are treated as untrusted data (separate folder, Python run with `-I`, nothing from them is executed).

## 8. Deviations
* **D1 (4 Nov 2026, after screening and the dry run, before any correlation was computed on benchmark data).** (a) Duplicate rule added: a series that shares at least one of its 12 selected samples with an earlier analysable series is excluded as a duplicate (GSE53183 and GSE53185 share all 12 samples; GSE53185 is excluded). (b) Screening yield: 25 (GPL570) and 39 (GPL1261) series were eligible among the 150 screened per platform (frozen list SHA-256 fc12de08...b950b9, 09:16); the dry run (DE gene counts only) found 18 analysable GPL570 series, 17 after the duplicate rule, and 20 GPL1261 series; the GPL570 list is exhausted at 17, so the target of 20 is not met and the list is not extended (37 series in total). (c) The grouping rules are mechanical, so some designs compare cell types, tissues or genotypes rather than drug treatments (for example GSE30164, GSE73265); all are kept because the question concerns group structure, and the designs are listed in `frozen_datasets.csv`.

## 9. Fixed query and parsing rules (added on 4 Nov 2026 before any candidate list was retrieved; only the result counts of the queries had been looked at: 1,388 series for GPL570 and 1,143 for GPL1261)
* Query (NCBI GEO DataSets, `db=gds`): `{GPL}[ACCN] AND gse[ETYP] AND 12:36[n_samples] AND (treated OR treatment OR knockdown OR inhibitor OR stimulated OR overexpression OR siRNA OR shRNA OR agonist OR cytokine)` with `{GPL}` = GPL570 or GPL1261. Accessions are sorted numerically, shuffled with `random.Random(2026)`, and the first 150 per platform are screened.
* Series matrix: `GSEnnn/GSExxxx/matrix/GSExxxx_series_matrix.txt.gz`; for multi-platform series the file whose name contains the platform accession. Only the header is read at the screening stage.
* Characteristics are parsed as `key: value` (split at the first colon). A key is a **perturbation key** if it contains one of the keywords of section 3 (case-insensitive). When several keys qualify, the first in file order is used. Title-based grouping: remove a trailing replicate token with `(?i)[\s_\-\.,#]*(rep(licate)?|sample|replic)?[\s_\-\.#]*\d{1,2}\s*$` and group identical remainders.
* Control-like labels are matched as whole words (case-insensitive): control, ctrl, vehicle, dmso, untreated, mock, nt, scrambled, wild, wt, naive, none, sham, pbs, saline, nontarget.
